Practice areas

What we do

Corporate and commercial work for foreign investors, financial institutions and developers, in priority order. Criminal and white-collar litigation is a real but secondary practice.

02Index
01Foreign direct investment & market entryApprovals, structuring and registration for foreign investors establishing operations in Nepal.
02Corporate & commercialCompany formation, governance, shareholder arrangements and the commercial contracts a business runs on.
03Banking & financeLending, security, regulatory compliance and recovery for banks, financial institutions and borrowers.
04Energy & infrastructureHydropower, transmission and infrastructure projects, from licensing through to power purchase and construction.
05Mergers & acquisitionsShare and asset transactions, due diligence, and the regulatory approvals a change of control requires.
06Employment & labourContracts, workplace policy, termination process and disputes under the Labour Act 2074.
07TaxCorporate tax, VAT, withholding, transfer pricing and disputes with the Inland Revenue Department.
08Intellectual propertyTrademark and patent registration, licensing, and enforcement against infringement.
09Dispute resolution & arbitrationCommercial litigation before the Nepali courts, domestic and international arbitration, and enforcement of awards.
10Criminal & white-collar litigationDefence in economic offence, banking offence and corruption proceedings, and related investigations.
