The firm, briefly

Stratum Law Associates advises corporate clients on Nepali law — foreign investment approval, company formation, banking and security, and the regulatory frameworks governing energy and infrastructure projects.

Criminal and white-collar litigation is a real but secondary practice, handled separately from the firm’s corporate work.
